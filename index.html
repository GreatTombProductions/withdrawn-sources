Did the source you're citing still stand?

Retractions, corrections and expressions of concern are public — but they are filed where the citing text isn't. Paste an article, claim, reading list, or bibliography here and this tool extracts every DOI and PMID it can find, checks each against the Retraction Watch Database snapshot, and shows you which sources carry update notices, with dates, reasons, and links. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Coverage: the Retraction Watch Database is the most complete public record of retractions, but it is not exhaustive, and corrections / expressions of concern are less comprehensively covered than retractions. No notice found is not proof that none exists. The snapshot is dated; see Method for limits, the snapshot receipt, and how to check beyond it. Data: Retraction Watch Database, made publicly available by Crossref (gitlab.com/crossref/retraction-watch-data); it is updated each working day by Retraction Watch.
